# ChatGPT Plus in China Without a Foreign Credit Card

[中文指南](README.md)

If you are in China and want ChatGPT Plus without a foreign credit card, first choose the access and payment route that fits your situation. The four common routes are OpenAI web checkout, an Apple App Store subscription, a third-party top-up to your own account, or a shared/rented account. Use an official route when it works for you. If you need Alipay, USDT, Chinese order lookup, or local support, verify the third-party service before paying.

A payment route does not change OpenAI's supported-country, account-eligibility, or regional-availability rules. Check the current [ChatGPT supported countries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663-chatgpt-supported-countries) and the checkout shown in your own account before attempting a purchase.

## Four routes at a glance

| Route | Best for | Check before paying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenAI web checkout | Users with a supported card and billing details | The account, currency, payment method, price, and renewal terms shown at checkout |
| Apple App Store | Users who already manage subscriptions through the correct Apple account and region | Store region, local price, renewal, and which Apple account owns the subscription |
| Third-party top-up to your own account | Users who need Alipay or USDT and want Chinese order support | No password handover, a traceable order, delivery rules, refund rules, and verification inside ChatGPT |
| Shared or rented account | Not recommended; included only for comparison | Account ownership, privacy, multi-user conflicts, and service interruption risk |

For private chats, work data, Codex projects, and long-term use, keep the subscription on an account you control. A low shared-seat price is not the same product as a subscription attached to your own account.

## Five checks for a third-party service

1. Confirm that the service clearly discloses that it is an independent third party, not OpenAI.
2. Never send a seller your password, email verification code, or recovery code. Treat Cookies, Tokens, API keys, and browser-session data as sensitive: do not send them through chat or to an unrelated site, and follow activation instructions only after verifying the intended domain and order.
3. Make sure you can look up the order and delivery status after payment.
4. Verify the activated plan inside your own ChatGPT account, not only from a seller screenshot.
5. Read the failure, delay, and refund rules before paying; do not create a second order while the first one is still being checked.
